Each one holds MechWarrior 3, Pirate's Moon, MechWarrior 1 Remake and the launcher (named BattleTech).

## Before you install

- **You need your own games.** No game files are included: the launcher sets each game up from your install, disc or disc image.
- **Upgrading from any earlier version:** install over it. Your pilots, saves, settings and mods are kept.
- **Mac:** the first time, macOS stops the app because it isn't signed. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
- **Linux:** make the AppImage executable, then run it.

## What's new

Fixes from your bug reports, for all three games.

- **Raw mouse:** the mouse moves exactly as far as you move it, without your system's acceleration (RAW MOUSE on the Upgrades page; MOUSE SENSITIVITY now goes to 5.0).
- **Key bindings and pads:** a newly bound key (the power key too) works at once; rumble pads take bindings again after losing focus; free look works on the middle mouse button.
- **Linux:** no crash or freeze at a mission's start or in the mech lab.
- Torso auto return with the mouse stays within its limit; Pirate's Moon started on its own asks for its own folder.
- **MechWarrior 1 Remake 1.0.5** (formerly MW1: Critical Hit): varied 'Mech prices, out-of-bounds lines on every map, arctic defence missions that can be lost.
