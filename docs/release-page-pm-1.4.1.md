Pirate's Moon on its own (mw3pm), without the launcher. The BattleTech launcher installs and updates it from this page.

**Most players want the launcher** (it installs MechWarrior 3, Pirate's Moon, MechWarrior 1 Remake, MechWarrior 1
Rebuild, The Crescent Hawk's Inception and The Crescent Hawks' Revenge and keeps them up to date): [the BattleTech launcher 2.2.0](https://github.com/telltaleatheist/mw3-re/releases/tag/v2.2.0).

## Download

- **Windows:** `PM-Rebuild-Setup-pm-v1.4.1.exe` (or `mw3pm-pm-v1.4.1-win64.zip`)
- **Mac:** `PM-Rebuild-pm-v1.4.1-mac.dmg`
- **Linux:** `PM-Rebuild-pm-v1.4.1-x86_64.AppImage`

No game files are included. Started without them, Pirate's Moon finds your copy (an install, a disc or a disc image),
or offers to download the original game files. Installing over an earlier version keeps your pilots, saves, settings
and mods.

## What's new

- **Windows zip copy: download 1.4.1 by hand this once** and use it in place of your copy (your pilots, saves,
  settings and mods are in your data folder and are kept). 1.4.0's in-game update could not move the game's folder and
  said the update didn't finish; from 1.4.1 on it works. The Windows Setup copy, the Mac and Linux were not affected.
- **A new version found at the start is a small notice in the corner** (UPDATE AVAILABLE, the version and the keys
  to press), not the update question over the game. The game runs on; the keys open the question.
- **A copy at the very top of a drive says up front that it can't update itself,** with the release page to download
  from.
