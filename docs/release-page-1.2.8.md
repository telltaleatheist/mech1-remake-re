Pirate's Moon on its own (mw3pm), without the launcher. The BattleTech launcher installs and updates it from this page.

**Most players want the launcher** (it installs MechWarrior 3, Pirate's Moon and MechWarrior 1 Remake and keeps them up to date): [MechWarrior 3 Rebuild 1.3.6](https://github.com/telltaleatheist/mw3-re/releases/tag/v1.3.6).

## Download

- **Windows:** `PM-Rebuild-Setup-pm-v1.2.8.exe` (or `mw3pm-pm-v1.2.8-win64.zip`)
- **Mac:** `PM-Rebuild-pm-v1.2.8-mac.dmg`
- **Linux:** `PM-Rebuild-pm-v1.2.8-x86_64.AppImage`

You need your own Pirate's Moon: no game files are included. Installing over an earlier version keeps your pilots, saves, settings and mods.
