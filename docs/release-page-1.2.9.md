Pirate's Moon on its own (mw3pm), without the launcher. The BattleTech launcher installs and updates it from this page.

**Most players want the launcher** (it installs MechWarrior 3, Pirate's Moon, MechWarrior 1 Remake and The Crescent Hawk's Inception and keeps them up to date): [MechWarrior 3 Rebuild 1.4.0](https://github.com/telltaleatheist/mw3-re/releases/tag/v1.4.0).

## Download

- **Windows:** `PM-Rebuild-Setup-pm-v1.2.9.exe` (or `mw3pm-pm-v1.2.9-win64.zip`)
- **Mac:** `PM-Rebuild-pm-v1.2.9-mac.dmg`
- **Linux:** `PM-Rebuild-pm-v1.2.9-x86_64.AppImage`

You need your own Pirate's Moon: no game files are included. Installing over an earlier version keeps your pilots, saves, settings and mods.

## What's new

- **Windows: your pilots, mods, music and saves from launchers up to 1.3.1 are back.** Those launchers put Pirate's Moon's folder inside MechWarrior 3's, and later versions didn't look there. The first start moves them to where the game looks. No file is ever written over.
- **ADD FROM MY DISC reads a .zip** of your disc image.
