# MechWarrior 3 Rebuild 1.5.2 (2026-11-25)

The mouse's settings in one place, and a launcher fix for the Mac.

## Changed

- **RAW MOUSE is on the Game Options screen, under MOUSE SENSITIVITY.** It was
  on the Upgrades page, where nobody looking for the mouse's speed would find
  it. ON reads the mouse's own movement, as the original did; OFF follows your
  system's pointer speed.
- **On the Mac, RAW MOUSE is off to start**, and this update switches it off
  once for everyone on a Mac (earlier versions saved it on for most players
  who never chose it): with it on, a Magic Mouse was several times too fast,
  even at the lowest MOUSE SENSITIVITY. Turn it back on if you liked it; your
  choice is kept from then on. Windows and Linux keep the setting they had.
- **MOUSE SENSITIVITY goes down to 0.1** (it stopped at 0.5), still up to 5.0.

## Fixed

- **On a Mac the Dock could show the launcher as a folder** ("Contents") after
  it updated itself. The launcher's update now keeps the app where it is and
  changes what is inside it. It takes effect from the update after this one;
  if your Dock shows a folder now, open the launcher from Applications once.
- The mission's mouse line in `mw3.log` gives the mission's own MOUSE
  SENSITIVITY (it always said 1.30).

## Versions

- Launcher and MechWarrior 3: 1.5.2.
- Pirate's Moon 1.2.10 and MechWarrior 1 Remake 1.1.2, with the same mouse
  changes. The Remake 1.1.2 also wakes the enemies on the two ice maps and
  brings them to you in battles and defences. The launcher also offers
  MechWarrior 1 Rebuild 1.0.1 and The Crescent Hawk's Inception 1.0.1.

### Upgrading

Install over 1.5.1, or press UPDATE AVAILABLE in your launcher. Your pilots,
saves, settings, mods and game files are kept.
