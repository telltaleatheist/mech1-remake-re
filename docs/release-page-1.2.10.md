Pirate's Moon on its own (mw3pm), without the launcher. The BattleTech launcher installs and updates it from this page.

**Most players want the launcher** (it installs MechWarrior 3, Pirate's Moon, MechWarrior 1 Remake and The Crescent Hawk's Inception and keeps them up to date): [MechWarrior 3 Rebuild 1.5.2](https://github.com/telltaleatheist/mw3-re/releases/tag/v1.5.2).

## Download

- **Windows:** `PM-Rebuild-Setup-pm-v1.2.10.exe` (or `mw3pm-pm-v1.2.10-win64.zip`)
- **Mac:** `PM-Rebuild-pm-v1.2.10-mac.dmg`
- **Linux:** `PM-Rebuild-pm-v1.2.10-x86_64.AppImage`

You need your own Pirate's Moon: no game files are included. Installing over an earlier version keeps your pilots, saves, settings and mods.

## What's new

- **RAW MOUSE is on the Game Options screen, under MOUSE SENSITIVITY** (it was on the Upgrades page). On the Mac it is off to start, and this update switches it off once there: a Magic Mouse was several times too fast with it. Turn it back on if you liked it; your choice is kept from then on.
- **MOUSE SENSITIVITY goes down to 0.1.**
