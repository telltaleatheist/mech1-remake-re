Pirate's Moon on its own (mw3pm), without the launcher. The BattleTech launcher installs and updates it from this page.

**Most players want the launcher** (it installs MechWarrior 3, Pirate's Moon, MechWarrior 1 Remake and The Crescent Hawk's Inception and keeps them up to date): [MechWarrior 3 Rebuild 1.6.0](https://github.com/telltaleatheist/mw3-re/releases/tag/v1.6.0).

## Download

- **Windows:** `PM-Rebuild-Setup-pm-v1.2.11.exe` (or `mw3pm-pm-v1.2.11-win64.zip`)
- **Mac:** `PM-Rebuild-pm-v1.2.11-mac.dmg`
- **Linux:** `PM-Rebuild-pm-v1.2.11-x86_64.AppImage`

You need your own Pirate's Moon: no game files are included. Installing over an earlier version keeps your pilots, saves, settings and mods.

## What's new

- **The mouse wheel scrolls the menus' lists** (issue #20): the Mech Lab's, the pilots, the saved games and the lobby's lists, and the commands on the controls screen. Under SMALL FIXES.
- **Pirate's Moon sets itself up:** finding your copy, installing it and adding its music are done by mw3pm itself, and the launcher shows its screens. With launcher 1.6.0, update Pirate's Moon to this version (UPDATE ALL).
- **PLAY and a start on its own open the same copy** when you have more than one.
