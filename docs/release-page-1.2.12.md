Pirate's Moon on its own (mw3pm), without the launcher. The BattleTech launcher installs and updates it from this page.

**Most players want the launcher** (it installs MechWarrior 3, Pirate's Moon, MechWarrior 1 Remake and The Crescent Hawk's Inception and keeps them up to date): [the BattleTech launcher 1.8.0](https://github.com/telltaleatheist/mw3-re/releases/tag/v1.8.0).

## Download

- **Windows:** `PM-Rebuild-Setup-pm-v1.2.12.exe` (or `mw3pm-pm-v1.2.12-win64.zip`)
- **Mac:** `PM-Rebuild-pm-v1.2.12-mac.dmg`
- **Linux:** `PM-Rebuild-pm-v1.2.12-x86_64.AppImage`

You need your own Pirate's Moon: no game files are included. Installing over an earlier version keeps your pilots, saves, settings and mods.

## What's new

- **On a Mac, Pirate's Moon opens like any other app:** the app and its download are signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS no longer stops it the first time.
- **The release's checksums are signed** (`SHA256SUMS.sig`), so the launcher can take Pirate's Moon from this project's mirror when GitHub cannot be reached.
- Nothing in play changes.

Full notes: [release-notes-1.6.1.md](https://github.com/telltaleatheist/mech3-re/blob/v1.6.1/docs/release-notes-1.6.1.md). `SHA256SUMS` and `SHA256SUMS.sig` are under Assets.
