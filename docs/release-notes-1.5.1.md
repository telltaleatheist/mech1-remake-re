# MechWarrior 3 Rebuild 1.5.1 (2026-11-25)

A fix for the launcher. MechWarrior 3 itself plays as in 1.5.0.

## Fixed

- **The launcher could use tens of gigabytes of memory on a Mac** while its
  window was covered, by a game played full screen for one: it drew as fast as
  it could. It now keeps to the display's pace, and to a few frames a second
  while covered or minimised.

## New in the launcher

- MechWarrior 1 Rebuild and The Crescent Hawk's Inception are found when
  installed by their own Windows Setups, and MechWarrior 1 Rebuild when it was
  started on its own. The launcher never takes one for the other.
- When one of them has found your game files but is not set up, its panel says
  so, and PLAY sets it up on the launcher's own screens first.

## Versions

- Launcher and MechWarrior 3: 1.5.1.
- Pirate's Moon 1.2.9 and MechWarrior 1 Remake 1.1.1 are unchanged. The launcher
  also offers MechWarrior 1 Rebuild 1.0.0 and The Crescent Hawk's Inception
  1.0.1.

### Upgrading

Install over 1.5.0, or press UPDATE AVAILABLE in your launcher. Your pilots,
saves, settings, mods and game files are kept.
