MechWarrior (1989): Elthial's SDL port (PLAY ORIGINAL)
=====================================================

The launcher's PLAY ORIGINAL on the MechWarrior 1 card starts this program,
which plays the 1989 game from your own copy of it. It is Elthial's work, not
ours: https://github.com/Elthial/Mechwarrior-sdl-port, built from commit
e119a72a6e7eed86d8d43d58a236e4212628fd56 with the build patches listed in
MechWarrior1989/licences/SOURCES.txt. Thank you, Elthial.

No part of MechWarrior (1989) is included. The program reads your own
installation; the launcher checks its BTECH.EXE is the revision the port plays.
